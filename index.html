    How the AI involvement scale works

    Each level needs reviewable evidence. Games stay unrated until enough has been checked.

    1. 0No evidence found. Not proof of no AI.
    2. 1Used as a development or marketing aid
    3. 2Limited in-game assets
    4. 3Assets across several in-game categories
    5. 4A major production component
    6. 5Runs at play time or is a core mechanic

    Official disclosures, reviewed third-party evidence and unverified community reports are always labeled separately. Level 0 is not a certification of zero AI use.

    Reporting a game? Include a source URL, its date, the affected content and context.
